Fixing Web3 Marketing
Without Selling User Data

A modular, ZK-proof-based growth funnel that enables personalised, wallet-native marketing — with zero identity leak.

Context
Web3 marketing is broken. Here's the fix.
Web3 platforms face a growth paradox: wallets are pseudonymous so targeting is impossible — but without it, growth is random airdrops and Discord spam. This proposes an ethical middle ground.

The Problem
3 broken patterns
No ethical targeting.
No trust. No personalisation.
Web3 platforms replicating Web2 invasive marketing tactics undermine the trust that decentralized ecosystems promise. The challenge: personalise without surveilling.
What makes Web3 marketing fail
Wallets are pseudonymous — standard identity-based targeting is impossible
Ads are perceived as scams due to poor UX and past phishing
Without data, experiences are flat and irrelevant — no personalisation
Airdrops are mercenary — they attract farmers, not advocates
The Opportunity
Wallet-native behavior is richer than cookie data — verifiable and specific
ZK proofs can verify attributes without exposing identity
On-chain reputation (soulbound NFTs) builds real community

The Solution: ZK Wallet Loop
User journey
01
Discover via ZK-targeted ad
User is shown an ad only because their wallet proves eligibility (e.g. "holds 3 NFTs", "staked ETH > 10 days") — without revealing who they are.
02
ZK proof unlocks offer
Wallet connects. ZK-SNARK verifies behavior attribute. Offer unlocks. No raw wallet data ever leaves the user's control.
03
Reward claimed → referral shared
Users earn tokens or soulbound NFTs. Referral loop begins. Status upgrades over time build real community loyalty — not mercenary airdrop behavior.
04
Admin monitors and iterates
dApp team sees wallet engagement, referral trees, and reward metrics. No identity. Full insight. Export to Dune or on-chain analytics platforms.

Key insight
Wallet-native signals are richer than cookie data. On-chain behavior is verifiable, specific, and consent-implied. Privacy vs personalisation is a false binary — not a trade-off.
