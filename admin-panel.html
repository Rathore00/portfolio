Indian SMBs struggle to fit global platforms to local needs. As the solo product designer, I led an admin panel from scratch, on a tight timeline.

The goal was an affordable, self-serve admin that reduces reliance on developers and plugins, and gives merchants deeper insight into customer behavior.

OwnedIA, wireframes, UX, high-fidelity UI, design system and prototyping
UsersSMBs, B2B and early-stage D2C founders, non-tech-savvy merchants
Worked withPM, developers, business head and marketing

Clients were frustrated by inflexibility and complexity. They did not want to depend on costly third-party plugins to adapt their store.

Hidden costsTransaction fees, app dependencies and premium features
Rigid checkoutLittle room to adapt the journey as the business grows
Overwhelming setupHard for merchants who are not tech-savvy
Content and commerceHybrid stores hit platform limits
No local optimizationGST, UPI and regional behavior are afterthoughts

Step 1DiscoveryStakeholder interviews on past Shopify setups, plus a competitive audit.
Step 2Synthesis and IAMapped friction in checkout, returns and product upload. Built a modular IA.
Step 3WireframingAtlassian Design System, internal reviews, and separate tabs for analytics and actions.
Step 4Design executionClean, minimal web UI with reusable components.
