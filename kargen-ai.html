A motion-led, interactive website for an AI workforce built for freight brokerages: a flipping hero, a bento grid, micro animations on text and effects on scroll.

The brief

Motion is hard to approve from a still frame

What Kargen asked for, and what it needed from the design.

Kargen asked for a motion-led, interactive website. The motion had to help busy freight brokers understand what Kargen does within seconds.

Developers also had to be able to replicate the motion easily. So the animations were judged in motion, early, with the real code running, instead of being imagined from a still frame.

What Kargen is

Kargen is an AI-powered automation platform built exclusively for freight brokerages. It gives small and mid-sized brokers an AI workforce that manages the carrier side of every load, from carrier sourcing and rate negotiation to dispatch, compliance verification and invoice settlement.
