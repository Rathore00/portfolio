Project overview

The Order-to-Cash Finance team managed Global Billing through a manually maintained workbook — "SFDC Metrics Report – Global Billing." The ask was straightforward: give Finance a way to understand billing operations without opening fifteen tabs. Not a visual refresh of Excel, but a working tool for operational decisions across Profiles and Settlements.

Key task

Context

The workbook already contained detailed operational data across four regions. The complexity came from how information was organized — every tab answered one predefined question, metrics were duplicated across regions, and weekly updates meant data was outdated by review time.

Solution

I reorganized the dataset into four decision-oriented sections — Overview, Action, Analysis – Closed Total, and Analysis – Totals by Status — with persistent faceted filters across Support Type, Region, Country, and Case Status.

Design decision

I deliberately chose four tabs instead of one dashboard

Combining all metrics into one page would have increased cognitive load, slowed performance, and reduced visibility of daily-use metrics.

The cost
One additional click
Users switch between four focused sections rather than scrolling a single overloaded page.
The benefit
Clear KPI hierarchy
Daily operational numbers stay immediately visible. Monthly and YTD analysis remains accessible without overwhelming the primary workflow.
The principle
Prioritize what teams need in the moment
Less about reducing clicks — more about preserving performant, decision-oriented information architecture.
